Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

HabitOS is a habit and goal tracking product from Veynum. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and the choices you have. If anything here is unclear, email us at hello@veynum.com.

Information we collect

  • Sign-in information. When you continue with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile photo from your Google account. We never see your Google password.
  • Your content. The habits, goals, milestones, tasks, notes, settings and progress you create in the app.
  • Account and plan details. Your plan (Free or Pro), subscription dates, and the notifications we show you about your plan or trial.
  • Checkout information. When you buy Pro we collect your name, Gmail address, WhatsApp number, selected plan and amount, payment method (bKash or Nagad) and the transaction ID you submit, along with the time of the order.
  • Basic technical data. Our hosting and database providers may process technical information such as IP address and device or browser details to deliver and secure the service.

How we use it

  • To provide HabitOS, keep you signed in and sync your data across your devices.
  • To verify your payment, activate Pro on the correct Google account and keep a record of the order.
  • To contact you about a payment or support request, including on WhatsApp if you gave us your number.
  • To show you messages about your plan, trial or account changes.
  • To protect the service and prevent misuse or duplicate payments.

We do not sell your personal information.

Where your data is stored and who processes it

HabitOS uses Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) to sign you in and store data, and Vercel to host the website and app. These providers process data on our behalf under their own terms and privacy policies. Your habits and goals are stored under your own account and are only readable by you; our administrators manage plans and payments but do not read your habit data in the app.

Cookies and local storage

We use your browser's local storage and similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember your theme and keep the app working offline. We do not use advertising cookies on this website.

Retention and deletion

We keep your account data while your account is active. Payment records are kept as long as needed to verify payments, resolve disputes and keep accurate records. You can ask us to delete your account and associated personal data by emailing hello@veynum.com from the email address on your account, and we will respond as soon as we reasonably can.

Your choices

  • You can edit or delete your habits and goals at any time inside the app.
  • You can ask us to correct or delete the information we hold about you.
  • You can sign out at any time and revoke HabitOS's access from your Google account settings.

Children

HabitOS is not intended for young children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the product changes. The date at the top shows when it was last updated. Continued use of HabitOS after a change means you accept the updated policy.
