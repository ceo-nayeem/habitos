FAQ

Questions, answered

How does payment work?

Choose a plan, enter your name and the Gmail you sign in with, then send the amount by bKash or Nagad to the number shown at checkout. Enter the transaction ID and submit. You'll see your order marked as received while we verify it.

When does Pro start?

We check payments manually. Once your transaction is verified, Pro is switched on for your Google account. You can follow the status any time on the order status page with your order reference.

Which Gmail should I use at checkout?

Use exactly the Google account you will sign in to HabitOS with. Pro is attached to that email. If you used a different email by mistake, contact support with your order reference and we'll fix it.

What happens when my Pro plan ends?

Your account moves back to the Free plan. Your habits, goals and history stay yours. Free limits apply to adding new items.

Can I renew or extend?

Yes. Buy another plan with the same Gmail and the time is added after your current Pro period.

Is my data private?

Your habits and goals are stored under your own account and are only readable by you. We use your Gmail to sign you in and to match your Pro plan.

Can I use it on my phone and laptop?

Yes. Sign in with Google on any device, and install HabitOS as an app from your browser.

What about refunds?

Please read our refund terms, or contact support if something went wrong with your payment.
